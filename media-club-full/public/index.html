About
FRAME 02/36

A collective.

MEDIA CLUB began in 2020 with three people, one camera and a shared Google Drive. It has since become the campus's main source of event photography and short-form video, all of it made by members.

Every piece is published with a byline. Your name stays on your work, in our archive, for as long as MEDIA CLUB exists.
